Auction House database

A complete logical model written as PostgreSQL DDL with 15 tables in third normal form. It covers participants and their roles, items and ownership history, appraisals, auctions, lots, bids, sales, fees and payments.

Constraints do the checking: date-range rules, unique open-period indexes, and a composite foreign key that guarantees a sale's winning bid belongs to the same lot.
